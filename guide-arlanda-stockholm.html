Arlanda to Stockholm: Prices, Times & How It Works

Distance, travel time, every transport option compared, and the real fixed prices — everything you need to plan your transfer.

The Quick Answer

Stockholm Arlanda Airport (ARN) sits about 40 km (25 miles) north of central Stockholm. A direct car transfer takes roughly 35–45 minutes depending on traffic and time of day — a bit longer during rush hour (7:30–9:00 and 16:00–18:00 on weekdays), a bit quicker late at night. There's no single "best" way to make the trip; it depends on your group size, luggage, and how much you value door-to-door convenience over the lowest possible per-person cost.

The train and bus prices above are per person — so for a family of four, the Arlanda Express alone can cost more than a fixed-price private transfer for the whole group, with door-to-door comfort and no lugging bags through a station.

Which Terminal Will I Land At?

Arlanda has four terminals. International flights (including most non-Schengen arrivals) typically use Terminal 5, while domestic and many European flights use Terminal 2 or Terminal 4. Terminal 3 handles some international arrivals as well. Whichever terminal you land at, share your flight number when booking and your driver will track it and meet you in the correct arrivals hall — you don't need to work out the terminal yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Arlanda Airport from Stockholm city center?

About 40 km (25 miles) north of central Stockholm — a direct car transfer takes roughly 35–45 minutes depending on traffic.

What is the cheapest way from Arlanda to Stockholm?

The Flygbussarna airport bus is usually the cheapest per-person option, followed by the Arlanda Express. For groups of two or more, a fixed-price private transfer is often cheaper per person than the train, since our price is per vehicle, not per passenger.

Is a private transfer more expensive than a taxi from Arlanda?

A fixed-price private transfer is usually cheaper and more predictable than a metered street taxi — the meter can run higher in heavy traffic, while our fixed price is agreed before you travel.
